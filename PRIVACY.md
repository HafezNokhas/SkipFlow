# SkipFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

SkipFlow is designed to work locally on the Android device. The current Beta does not request Internet access and does not automatically upload data.

## Accessibility use

SkipFlow uses Android Accessibility only inside the official YouTube app. It looks for YouTube's official enabled skip-ad control and requests one click when the control passes the app's safety checks.

## Data SkipFlow does not collect

SkipFlow does not collect or store:

- full screen text or screenshots;
- video or advertising content;
- Google or YouTube account information;
- email addresses or passwords;
- permanent device identifiers or advertising IDs;
- browsing history, location, contacts, camera, or microphone data.

## Local diagnostics

SkipFlow keeps a small local diagnostic log containing safe state codes, anonymous aggregate counters, sanitized app crash frames, device manufacturer/model, Android and app versions, service status, and Android process-exit reasons when the operating system provides them. These diagnostics are used to troubleshoot reliability problems. They do not include screen content or account data.

The user controls whether to export or share a diagnostic report. Nothing is sent automatically. The local diagnostic history can be cleared from the app.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the Beta changes. Updates will be published in this repository.
