# Troubleshooting

## SkipFlow says the service is not connected

Open Android **Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps/services → SkipFlow**. Turn the service off and on again, then return to SkipFlow.

## The service stops after locking the phone or clearing recent apps

Some manufacturers restrict background services. Open SkipFlow's Android app settings and review Battery or Background usage. On Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO devices, **No restrictions**, Background autostart, and keeping SkipFlow in Recent apps may improve reliability. These settings remain under your control.

## The skip button is not pressed

- Confirm that Automation is on and the service is connected.
- Confirm that you are using the official YouTube Android app.
- SkipFlow acts only on the official enabled Skip button. It does not bypass countdowns or unskippable ads.
- YouTube interface updates can temporarily affect detection.

## Installation is blocked

Allow installation from the browser or file manager that downloaded the APK. Android may show a warning because this Beta is installed outside Google Play.

## The problem continues

Open **Support & Diagnostics**, run the diagnostic test, and export the report. Follow the [support instructions](../SUPPORT.md).
