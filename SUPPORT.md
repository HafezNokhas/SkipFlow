# SkipFlow Support

SkipFlow is a limited Beta. Before asking for help, follow the [troubleshooting guide](docs/troubleshooting.md).

If the problem continues:

1. Open SkipFlow.
2. Open **Support & Diagnostics**.
3. Tap **Run Diagnostic Test**.
4. Tap **Export Diagnostic Report** and save the text file.
5. Open a GitHub issue with a short description of what happened, your Android version, and your phone manufacturer/model.

Do not post passwords, account information, screenshots, or a full diagnostic report in a public GitHub issue. Share the exported report only through a private channel agreed with the developer.

[Open a support issue](https://github.com/HafezNokhas/SkipFlow/issues/new)
